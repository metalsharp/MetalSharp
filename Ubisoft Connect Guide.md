This Guide explains how to use Ubisoft Connect from MetalSharp; 

1) Install Ubisoft Connect from the 'Start Steam' Dropdown button located in the main library header. MetalSharp will create a
Ubisoft-Prefix and launch the installer. Install it to it's default recommended location to ensure MetalSharp binds to it correctly
for game launches.
___

3) One installed, it will become available as 'Launch Ubisoft' from the dropdown:

<img width="370" height="117" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-28 at 10 08 12 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aba24c45-a35b-49ad-bfb9-e34f9de0a094" />

Click it at any time to open Ubisoft Connect. Sequentially, it can be stopped from the same dropdown: 

<img width="370" height="117" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-28 at 10 00 17 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d32c82f1-468c-4622-a1fa-6dde3265dfc2" />

___

3) You can then login and install your first game. MetalSharp maps external drives to Ubisoft Connect using a Y: Mapping.
You can change the install location to this drive by clicking on 'Change Location' when installing a game, selecting the visible drive with a `/` and selecting Volumes->External Drive Name-> and creating a folder there called `Ubisoft`:

<img width="370" height="356" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-28 at 10 35 24 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/44c1cbfe-ff46-4878-a2e4-49245b7059fb" />

<img width="370" height="365" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-28 at 10 35 55 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/688a0584-1884-4be9-9144-0a06121a7333" />

<img width="370" height="365" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-28 at 10 36 17 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/21ae94ab-6d2c-47e6-8939-cf566c4a7e51" />

___

4) Games that are installed with Ubisoft Connect will automatically appear in your main library. Select from your desired launch pipeline, and
launch the game. You will also be able to stop Ubisoft Connect games with cmd + opt + q or using the `Stop` button from within MetalSharp. Ubisoft Connect does not need to be started to launch a Ubisoft Connect installed game, it will launch automatically when started:

<img width="1147" height="877" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-28 at 9 59 53 AM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/830803d3-575b-4964-b661-714a4e7db632" />

That's it! Enjoy the new integration!
